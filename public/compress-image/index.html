Image compressor

Reduce the file size of JPG, PNG and WebP images in one place.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to compress images

  1. Choose your images or drop them in the box.
  2. Pick a quality and an output format. For PNG, choose WebP or JPG for the biggest savings.
  3. Set a max width if the images are larger than you need.
  4. Download each image or all of them as a ZIP.

JPG and WebP compress well by lowering quality. PNG is lossless, so the biggest savings come from saving it as WebP or JPG instead. WebP is supported by all modern browsers and is usually the smallest choice for websites.

Questions

Why did my PNG barely shrink?

PNG keeps every pixel, so re-saving it as PNG saves little. Choose WebP or JPG as the output format to make it much smaller.

Does compressing remove transparency?

WebP and PNG keep transparency. JPG does not support it, so transparent areas become white.

Which format is smallest?

WebP is usually 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality.

Is this image compressor safe?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

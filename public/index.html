Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on LocalFileKit is free with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit. The site is supported by ads.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser on your own device. Your files are never sent to a server.

Do the tools work on phones?

Yes. They work in modern browsers on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Chromebook.

Can I download YouTube videos here?

No. These tools work with files you already have on your device.

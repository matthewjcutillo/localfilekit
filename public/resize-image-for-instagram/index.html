How to resize an image for Instagram

  1. Choose your image or drop it in the box.
  2. Keep the preset size of 1080 px wide, or change it.
  3. Download the resized image and upload it.

Instagram shows posts at 1080 px wide. Square posts are 1080 × 1080, portrait posts 1080 × 1350. Setting only the width keeps your photo’s shape, so nothing is stretched.

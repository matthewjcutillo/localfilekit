Convert WAV to MP3

Make big WAV recordings small enough to email, share or store.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to convert WAV to MP3

  1. Choose your WAV files or drop them in the box.
  2. Pick the MP3 quality.
  3. Download the MP3 files.

A 5-minute stereo WAV is about 50 MB. As a 192 kbps MP3 it is about 7 MB and sounds the same to most listeners.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Does WAV to MP3 lose quality?

MP3 is compressed, but at 256–320 kbps the difference is very hard to hear.

Can I convert other audio files?

Yes. FLAC, OGG, AAC and M4A files work too, depending on your browser.

Are my files uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

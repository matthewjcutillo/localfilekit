Convert JPG to PDF

Combine photos, scans and screenshots into a single PDF.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to convert JPG to PDF

  1. Choose your images or drop them in the box.
  2. Use the arrows to set the page order.
  3. Pick a page size and margin.
  4. Click "Create PDF" to download it.

Great for sending photographed receipts, IDs or signed forms as one tidy document. Pick A4 or US Letter for pages that print well, or keep each page the size of its image.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Which image types work?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP. Each image becomes one page.

Will the PDF print well?

Choose A4 or US Letter with a small margin for clean printing.

Are my images uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

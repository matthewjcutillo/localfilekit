Convert MP4 to MOV

Turn MP4 videos into MOV files that play where you need them.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to convert MP4 to MOV

  1. Choose your MP4 video or drop it in the box.
  2. Leave "MOV" selected.
  3. Keep the tab open while it converts.
  4. Download your MOV.

Editing on a Mac? MOV is Apple’s video format and opens cleanly in QuickTime, iMovie and Final Cut.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Does MP4 to MOV lower the quality?

The video is re-encoded at a high quality setting, so the difference is hard to see.

How long does it take?

About as long as the video itself on a typical laptop. Short clips are quick; long videos are faster on a computer than a phone.

Are my videos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

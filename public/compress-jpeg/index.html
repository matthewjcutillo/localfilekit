Compress JPEG images

Make JPG photos smaller for email, websites and forms while keeping them sharp.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to compress JPEG images

  1. Choose your JPG photos or drop them in the box.
  2. Pick a quality. 75% is a good balance of size and sharpness.
  3. Optionally set a max width to make large photos smaller still.
  4. Download each photo, or all of them as a ZIP.

Phone photos are often 3–8 MB. Most of that size is detail the eye cannot see. Setting quality around 70–80% usually cuts the file by half or more, and setting a max width (for example 1920 px) cuts it further.

Questions

How much smaller will my JPEG get?

Usually 40–80% smaller, depending on the photo and the quality you pick. If a photo is already well compressed, the original is kept so it never gets bigger.

What quality should I choose?

75% works for most photos. Use 85–90% for prints or detailed images and 60% when size matters most, like email attachments.

Is there a file size or count limit?

No fixed limit. Very large batches depend on your device memory.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

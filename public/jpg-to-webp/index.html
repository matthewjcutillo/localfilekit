Convert JPG to WebP

Turn JPG images into WebP files in seconds, one at a time or in a batch.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to convert JPG to WebP

  1. Choose your JPG files or drop them in the box.
  2. Check that "WebP" is selected and set the quality.
  3. Download each WebP file, or all of them as a ZIP.

WebP is usually 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality, which helps web pages load faster and can improve search rankings.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Will converting JPG to WebP lose quality?

WebP is a compressed format. At 85–95% quality the difference is very hard to see.

Can I convert many JPG files at once?

Yes. Select or drop as many as you like and download them together as a ZIP.

Are my images uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

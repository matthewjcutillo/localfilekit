Resize images

Change image dimensions in pixels or by percentage, for one photo or a whole folder.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to resize images

  1. Choose your images or drop them in the box.
  2. Choose pixels or percent and enter the size you want.
  3. Keep the original format or switch to JPG, PNG or WebP.
  4. Download the resized images.

Enter just a width or just a height and the other side is worked out for you, so nothing gets stretched. Enter both to set an exact size.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

How do I keep the aspect ratio?

Fill in only the width or only the height. The other side is calculated automatically.

Can I make an image bigger?

Yes, but enlarging cannot add detail, so very large increases will look soft.

What size should I use for Instagram or a website?

Instagram posts look best at 1080 px wide. For most websites, 1600–1920 px wide is plenty.

Are my images uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

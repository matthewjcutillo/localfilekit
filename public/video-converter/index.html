Convert videos to MP4, WebM or MOV without installing anything.

How to video converter

  1. Choose a video or drop it in the box.
  2. Pick the format to convert to.
  3. Keep the tab open while it converts; the progress bar shows how far along it is.
  4. Download the converted video.

MP4 with H.264 plays on nearly every phone, computer, TV and social site, so it is the safest choice. Conversion happens on your device, so speed depends on your computer and the length of the video.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Is there a watermark?

No. Your video is converted as is, with no watermark and no time limit.

Why is it slower than desktop software?

The converter runs inside your browser for privacy. Short clips convert quickly; long or 4K videos take longer.

What is the size limit?

Videos under about 500 MB work best. Larger files depend on your device memory.

Are my videos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

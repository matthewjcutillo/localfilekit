Extract audio from video

Turn the soundtrack of a MOV, WebM, MKV or AVI video into an MP3 or WAV.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to extract audio from video

  1. Choose your video files or drop them in the box.
  2. Pick MP3 or WAV, and the MP3 quality.
  3. Wait while the audio is extracted.
  4. Download the audio file.

Most videos convert instantly. Less common formats are handled by a built-in converter that loads the first time you need it, then stays cached in your browser.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Which video formats work?

MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM, MKV, AVI, FLV, WMV and 3GP in most cases.

Why did the first conversion take longer?

Some formats need the full converter, about 30 MB, which downloads once and is then cached by your browser.

Are my videos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

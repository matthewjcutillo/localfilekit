Merge PDF files

Combine several PDFs into one document in the order you choose.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to merge PDF files

  1. Choose your PDF files or drop them in the box.
  2. Use the arrows to put them in the right order.
  3. Click "Merge PDFs".
  4. Your merged PDF downloads automatically.

Useful for putting together applications, invoices, contracts and scanned pages. Because nothing is uploaded, it is safe for private documents.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Is there a limit to how many PDFs I can merge?

No fixed limit. Very large files depend on your device memory.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password first in the app you use to open it.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

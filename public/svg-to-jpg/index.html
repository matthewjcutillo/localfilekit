Convert SVG to JPG

Turn SVG images into JPG files in seconds, one at a time or in a batch.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to convert SVG to JPG

  1. Choose your SVG files or drop them in the box.
  2. Check that "JPG" is selected and set the quality.
  3. Download each JPG file, or all of them as a ZIP.

Turn an SVG logo or drawing into a JPG that any app can open. Transparent areas become white.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Will converting SVG to JPG lose quality?

JPG is a compressed format. At 85–95% quality the difference is very hard to see.

Can I convert many SVG files at once?

Yes. Select or drop as many as you like and download them together as a ZIP.

Are my images uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

Rotate PDF pages

Fix sideways or upside-down scans and save the PDF the right way up.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to rotate PDF pages

  1. Pick the rotation and, if needed, which pages.
  2. Choose your PDF or drop it in the box.
  3. Download the rotated PDF.

Rotate every page, or type page numbers like "2, 5-6" to rotate only those.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Is the rotation saved permanently?

Yes. The downloaded PDF opens with the new rotation in every PDF reader.

Can I rotate just one page?

Yes. Type its page number in the Pages box before choosing the file.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

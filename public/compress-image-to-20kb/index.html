Compress an image to 20 KB

Drop in a photo and get a copy under 20 KB, with the best quality that still fits.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to compress an image to 20 KB

  1. Choose your photos or drop them in the box.
  2. Leave the target at 20 KB, or type a different size.
  3. Download each photo under 20 KB, or all of them as a ZIP.

Some government and exam forms only accept photos and signatures under 20 KB. Instead of guessing a quality setting, this tool tries different settings for you and keeps the sharpest version that comes in under 20 KB. If a photo is very large, it also scales it down a little until it fits.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Will the photo be exactly 20 KB?

It will be 20 KB or a little under. The tool picks the highest quality that still fits the limit.

Why did my image get smaller in pixels?

Some photos cannot reach 20 KB by quality alone. Then the tool shrinks the width and height step by step until the file fits.

Can I do many photos at once?

Yes. Every photo you add is fitted to the same size, and you can download them together as a ZIP.

Are my photos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

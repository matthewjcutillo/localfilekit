Compress an image to 500 KB

Drop in a photo and get a copy under 500 KB, with the best quality that still fits.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to compress an image to 500 KB

  1. Choose your photos or drop them in the box.
  2. Leave the target at 500 KB, or type a different size.
  3. Download each photo under 500 KB, or all of them as a ZIP.

A 500 KB limit is common on forums, school portals and listing sites. Instead of guessing a quality setting, this tool tries different settings for you and keeps the sharpest version that comes in under 500 KB. If a photo is very large, it also scales it down a little until it fits.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Will the photo be exactly 500 KB?

It will be 500 KB or a little under. The tool picks the highest quality that still fits the limit.

Why did my image get smaller in pixels?

Some photos cannot reach 500 KB by quality alone. Then the tool shrinks the width and height step by step until the file fits.

Can I do many photos at once?

Yes. Every photo you add is fitted to the same size, and you can download them together as a ZIP.

Are my photos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

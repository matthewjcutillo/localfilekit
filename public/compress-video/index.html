Compress video

Shrink videos so they fit email, WhatsApp, Discord and upload limits.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to compress video

  1. Choose a video or drop it in the box.
  2. Pick how strong the compression should be.
  3. Pick a max resolution. 720p suits most sharing.
  4. Download the smaller MP4.

Lowering the resolution to 720p and using medium compression usually makes a phone video 3–10 times smaller while still looking good on a phone screen.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

How small will my video get?

It depends on the original. Phone videos at 1080p or 4K often shrink 70–90% at 720p with medium compression.

Which setting should I use for Discord or email?

Choose Strong and 480p or 720p to get under 25 MB for most short clips.

Are my videos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

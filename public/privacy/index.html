Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Your files

All tools on LocalFileKit process files locally in your browser. Files you choose are not uploaded to our servers or to anyone else, and we cannot see them. When you close the page, they are gone from the page’s memory.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use this to identify you.

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

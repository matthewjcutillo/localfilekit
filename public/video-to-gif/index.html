Video to GIF maker

Turn a short moment from a video into an animated GIF.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to video to GIF maker

  1. Choose a video or drop it in the box.
  2. Set where the GIF starts and how long it lasts.
  3. Pick the frame rate and width.
  4. Download your GIF.

GIFs get large quickly, so keep them short: 3–6 seconds at 480 px wide and 10 fps is a good size for chats and websites.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

How long can the GIF be?

Up to 60 seconds, but shorter GIFs are much smaller and load faster.

Why is my GIF bigger than the video?

GIF is an old format with weak compression. Lower the width, frame rate or length to make it smaller.

Are my videos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

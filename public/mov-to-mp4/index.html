How to convert MOV to MP4

  1. Choose your MOV video or drop it in the box.
  2. Leave "MP4 (H.264)" selected.
  3. Keep the tab open while it converts.
  4. Download your MP4.

MOV files from iPhones often use codecs that Windows and some websites will not open. MP4 with H.264 is the most widely supported video format.

Convert MOV to MP3

Turn MOV files into MP3 audio in a few seconds, right on your device.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to convert MOV to MP3

  1. Choose your MOV files or drop them in the box.
  2. Pick MP3 quality. 192 kbps suits most uses; 320 kbps for music.
  3. Wait while the audio is converted.
  4. Download your MP3 file.

MOV is the video format iPhones and Macs record in. Pull the sound out of an iPhone video, rehearsal or interview as an MP3.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

What MP3 quality should I choose?

128 kbps is fine for speech, 192 kbps for most music and 320 kbps for the best quality.

Can I convert several MOV files at once?

Yes. Add as many as you like and they are converted one after another.

Are my files uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

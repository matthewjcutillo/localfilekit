Image converter

Convert images between JPG, PNG and WebP, one at a time or in bulk.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to image converter

  1. Choose your images or drop them in the box.
  2. Pick the format to convert to.
  3. Set the quality for JPG or WebP.
  4. Download each file or all of them as a ZIP.

Use JPG for photos, PNG for graphics and screenshots that need sharp edges or transparency, and WebP when you want the smallest file for the web.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

Which formats can I convert from?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF (first frame), BMP, AVIF and SVG, as long as your browser can open them.

Can I convert HEIC iPhone photos?

Only in browsers that can open HEIC, such as Safari on Mac and iPhone. On other browsers, set your iPhone camera to "Most Compatible" to save JPGs.

Are my images uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

Split PDF

Pull out just the pages you need, or break a PDF into single pages.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to split PDF

  1. Choose a PDF or drop it in the box.
  2. Pick "Extract pages" and type the pages, or pick "Every page".
  3. Click "Split PDF".
  4. Download your new PDF or the ZIP of pages.

Type pages and ranges like "1-3, 7, 10-" to build a new PDF from just those pages. Leaving out pages is also an easy way to delete them.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

How do I delete pages from a PDF?

Extract every page except the ones you want to delete. For example, to remove page 4 from a 10-page file, type "1-3, 5-10".

What does "10-" mean?

It means page 10 to the last page.

Are my PDFs uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.

Convert MP4 to MP3

Save the sound from your own MP4 videos as an MP3 you can play anywhere.

🔒 Private: files stay on your device

How to convert MP4 to MP3

  1. Choose your MP4 files or drop them in the box.
  2. Pick MP3 quality. 192 kbps is great for most uses; 320 kbps for music.
  3. Wait a few seconds while the audio is converted.
  4. Download your MP3.

Handy for turning a recorded rehearsal, lecture, voice memo or interview into an audio file for your phone or podcast app. Use this only with videos you own or have the right to use.

Free, private and no sign-up

LocalFileKit runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are processed on your own device and are never sent to a server, so they stay private and there are no upload waits, no account and no watermark.

Questions

What MP3 quality should I choose?

128 kbps is fine for speech, 192 kbps for most music, and 320 kbps for the best quality.

Can I get a WAV file instead?

Yes. Choose WAV as the output for uncompressed audio, useful for editing.

Can I convert YouTube videos here?

No. This tool works with video files already on your device. Downloading from YouTube goes against its terms.

Are my videos uploaded?

Your files never leave your device. Everything runs inside your browser, so there is no upload, no waiting in a queue and nothing stored on a server.
